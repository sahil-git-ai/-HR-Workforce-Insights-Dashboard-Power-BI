# -HR-Workforce-Insights-Dashboard-Power-BI
📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard analyzes HR data of 1,000 employees across 7 departments and 3 countries — United States, China, and Brazil. The goal was to help HR teams understand workforce distribution, salary gaps, attrition patterns, and diversity metrics for better people management decisions.

📊 Key Metrics Tracked
Metric                  Value
Total Employees         1,000

Countries                 3(USA, China, Brazil)

🔍 Key Insights from the Dashboard

Largest Department: IT leads with 241 employees (24.1%) — highest headcount and highest salary, making it the most resource-heavy department
Salary Gap Identified: IT and Engineering earn significantly more than Accounting — gap visible in salary analysis bar chart, useful for compensation review
Ethnicity Distribution: Caucasian employees form the largest group (36.6% = 366), followed by Latino (25.2% = 252) and Asian (23.3% = 233) — useful for diversity & inclusion tracking
Attrition Risk: Exit date data shows hiring peaked in early years — long-tenure employees in mid-level departments may be at retention risk
Global Workforce: Employee distribution across North America, Asia, and South America — map visual shows concentration in US cities
Gender Balance: Near equal split between male and female employees across departments


🛠️ Tools & Techniques Used

Tool: Microsoft Power BI Desktop
Visuals Used: Pie chart, bar chart, donut chart, Bing map, slicers
Slicers: Gender, Department, Country, Business Unit filter
DAX Measures: COUNT, SUM, CALCULATE for KPI cards
Map Visual: Employee location plotted by city and country using Power BI Bing Maps


💼 Business Use Case
This dashboard is built for HR managers and CHROs to:

Monitor headcount and department-wise employee distribution
Identify salary gaps across job levels and departments
Track diversity metrics for inclusion reporting
Analyze hire vs exit trends for workforce planning
